Research ledger

An investigation keeps its analysis and its verification on the Board, so that a later Case can read what was tried and what it showed.

Each idea to test is a hypothesis with its Case. The numbers an argument rests on are data, each naming where it came from and its Case. A hypothesis is decided by a rule that derives it supported or refuted from the receipts and data that settle it; a direction given up is dropped with a reason.

A hypothesis dropped with a reason is dropped.
A hypothesis stays open until it is supported, refuted or dropped.
A hypothesis a rule derives as supported is shown as supported.
A hypothesis a rule derives as refuted is shown as refuted.
A dropped hypothesis is shown as dropped.
A Case with an open hypothesis still has an open question.
Each datum recorded for a Case is shown with that Case.
